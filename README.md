# Datheon Data Analytics & Power BI Internship

## About This Repository

This repository contains my projects completed during the Datheon Data Analytics & Power BI Internship.

The projects demonstrate practical skills in data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, business intelligence, data visualization, and customer analytics.

## Projects

### Task 1: Superstore Sales EDA
- Data cleaning using Python and Pandas.
- Customer purchase analysis.
- Monthly sales seasonality.
- Category-wise sales and profit.
- Discount sensitivity analysis.
- Executive business insights.

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook.

### Task 2: Interactive Power BI Executive Sales Dashboard
- Sales and profit KPI cards.
- Star Schema data modeling.
- DAX measures.
- Monthly sales trends.
- Regional performance.
- Product profitability analysis.

**Tools:** Power BI, Power Query, DAX.

### Task 3: Customer Cohort and Churn Analytics
- Customer churn rate analysis.
- Tenure cohort analysis.
- Contract and payment method analysis.
- High-risk customer identification.
- Customer retention insights.

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Seaborn, Power BI.
